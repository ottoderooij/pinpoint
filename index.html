Where on Earth is this?

How to play
  • You get 5 satellite images, starting zoomed in close.
  • Stuck? Tap Zoom out to see more, but each zoom-out costs points.
  • Tap Guess, drop a pin on the world map, and confirm.
  • The closer your pin, the more points (max 1000 per round).
